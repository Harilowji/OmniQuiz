[TITLE]: Digital SAT Math Practice Exam
[DESCRIPTION]: 10 official style Digital SAT Math questions covering Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem Solving, and Geometry.
[TIME]: 15 minutes

Câu 1: If $3x + 7 = 22$, what is the value of $6x - 5$?
A. 25
B. 30
C. 15
D. 20
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: First, solve for $x$: $3x = 22 - 7 = 15 \Rightarrow x = 5$. Then substitute $x$ into $6x - 5$: $6(5) - 5 = 30 - 5 = 25$.

Câu 2: A line in the $xy$-plane passes through the points $(2, 5)$ and $(4, 11)$. What is the slope of this line?
A. 3
B. 2
C. 6
D. $\frac{1}{3}$
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: The slope $m$ is given by $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{11 - 5}{4 - 2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3$.

Câu 3: Which of the following is equivalent to $(2x^2 - 3x + 4) - (x^2 + 2x - 5)$?
A. $x^2 - 5x + 9$
B. $x^2 - x - 1$
C. $3x^2 - x - 1$
D. $x^2 - 5x - 1$
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: Distribute the negative sign: $2x^2 - 3x + 4 - x^2 - 2x + 5 = (2x^2 - x^2) + (-3x - 2x) + (4 + 5) = x^2 - 5x + 9$.

Câu 4: The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 11$. What is the minimum value of $f(x)$?
A. 3
B. 2
C. 11
D. -3
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: The vertex of the parabola $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ occurs at $x = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{-8}{2(2)} = 2$. Evaluating at $x = 2$: $f(2) = 2(2)^2 - 8(2) + 11 = 8 - 16 + 11 = 3$. Since $a = 2 > 0$, this is the minimum value.

Câu 5: If $4^{x+1} = 64$, what is the value of $x$?
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 1
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: Rewrite 64 with base 4: $64 = 4^3$. Therefore, $4^{x+1} = 4^3 \Rightarrow x + 1 = 3 \Rightarrow x = 2$.

Câu 6: A circle in the $xy$-plane has equation $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 49$. What are the coordinates of the center and the radius of the circle?
A. Center $(3, -4)$, Radius 7
B. Center $(-3, 4)$, Radius 7
C. Center $(3, -4)$, Radius 49
D. Center $(-3, 4)$, Radius 49
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: Standard equation of a circle is $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$. Here $h = 3$, $k = -4$, and $r = \sqrt{49} = 7$.

Câu 7: A store offers a $20\%$ discount on a jacket originally priced at $\$80$. If an additional $5\%$ sales tax is applied to the discounted price, what is the final price?
A. $\$67.20$
B. $\$64.00$
C. $\$68.00$
D. $\$66.40$
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: Discounted price = $\$80 \times (1 - 0.20) = \$64.00$. With 5% sales tax: $\$64.00 \times 1.05 = \$67.20$.

Câu 8: If $\frac{a}{b} = 2$, what is the value of $\frac{4b}{a}$?
A. 2
B. 4
C. $\frac{1}{2}$
D. 8
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: Since $\frac{a}{b} = 2$, it follows that $\frac{b}{a} = \frac{1}{2}$. Therefore, $\frac{4b}{a} = 4 \times \frac{b}{a} = 4 \times \frac{1}{2} = 2$.

Câu 9: In right triangle $ABC$, the measure of angle $C$ is $90^\circ$, and $\sin A = \frac{3}{5}$. What is the value of $\cos A$?
A. $\frac{4}{5}$
B. $\frac{3}{4}$
C. $\frac{5}{3}$
D. $\frac{5}{4}$
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: Using the Pythagorean trigonometric identity $\sin^2 A + \cos^2 A = 1$, we have $\cos A = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 A} = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{9}{25}} = \sqrt{\frac{16}{25}} = \frac{4}{5}$.

Câu 10: The system of equations $2x + 3y = 7$ and $kx + 6y = 15$ has no solution. What is the value of $k$?
A. 4
B. 6
C. 2
D. -4
Đáp án: A
Lời giải: For a system of two linear equations to have no solution (parallel lines), the ratio of coefficients of $x$ and $y$ must be equal, but not equal to the constant term: $\frac{k}{2} = \frac{6}{3} \neq \frac{15}{7}$. Since $\frac{6}{3} = 2$, we have $\frac{k}{2} = 2 \Rightarrow k = 4$.
